📊 Project Overview:
This project focuses on analyzing Jumia's e-commerce data using Microsoft Power BI to explore business performance across sales, profitability, orders, products,
customers, sellers, marketing, and logistics.
The goal is to transform raw data into an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard that provides meaningful insights and supports data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Project Objectives:
Monitor overall sales and business performance.
Analyze revenue, costs, and profitability.
Understand customer purchasing behavior.
Evaluate product and category performance.
Analyze seller performance.
Explore marketing channel effectiveness.
Monitor logistics and delivery performance.
Identify business trends and performance opportunities.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies:
Microsoft Power BI
Power Query
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
Data Cleaning & Transformation
Data Modeling
Data Visualization
Interactive Dashboards

📈 Key Analysis Areas:
1. Sales & Business Performance
Analyze sales revenue, costs, profit, and orders.
Track key business performance indicators.
Identify sales trends and performance patterns.
2. Product Analysis
Evaluate product and category performance.
Identify high-performing products and categories.
Compare sales performance across different products.
3. Customer Analysis
Explore customer purchasing behavior.
Analyze customer demographics.
Identify patterns in customer activity.
4. Seller Performance
Compare seller performance.
Evaluate sales contributions across sellers.
5. Marketing Analysis
Analyze marketing channels.
Explore the relationship between marketing activities and sales performance.
6. Logistics Analysis
Monitor delivery-related metrics.
Explore logistics performance and delivery patterns.

📌 Dashboard Features:
Interactive visualizations.
KPI cards for monitoring business performance.
Dynamic filters and slicers.
Multiple analytical views.
Data-driven reporting to support business decisions.

💡 Business Value:
This dashboard provides a consolidated view of e-commerce performance and helps identify important trends across sales, products, customers, sellers, marketing,
and logistics.
The insights can support better business decisions, improve performance monitoring, and highlight potential areas for improvement.
